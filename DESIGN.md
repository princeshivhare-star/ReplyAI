# ReplyAI interface

ReplyAI is a calm, approachable workspace for small teams managing customer conversations. The interface should feel clear and dependable rather than technical or overly playful.

## Visual system

- Canvas: `#f7f8fa`; cards and navigation: white; primary text: `#18212b`; secondary text: `#77818c`.
- ReplyAI violet: `#7264e8`; use it for the primary action, selected navigation, and small highlights. Green indicates a healthy connection or completed setup.
- Use system sans-serif text with restrained weight and spacing. Reserve strong display type for short page headings.
- Prefer thin neutral borders, modest 8–12px corner radii, and whitespace over gradients, shadows, or decorative imagery.
- Keep forms, status indicators, and confirmation/error messages explicit and accessible.

## Layout and behavior

- Desktop uses a persistent left workspace navigation and a wide content area.
- On narrow screens, navigation becomes a horizontally scrollable row; content cards stack and actions remain reachable.
- Color is never the only way to communicate status. Use text and accessible labels with status indicators.
- Keep social account tokens and other secrets out of the browser UI.
